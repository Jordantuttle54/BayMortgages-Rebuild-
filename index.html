When to speak to a mortgage adviser

Is It the Right Time to Speak to a Mortgage Adviser?

Many people assume they need to have everything ready before speaking to a mortgage adviser. In reality, the earlier you get advice, the easier the process becomes. If any of the situations below sound familiar, it may be the perfect time to have a quick conversation.

You're thinking about buying your first home

Understanding borrowing limits, deposit requirements and lender expectations early can make the entire buying process far smoother.

Your fixed mortgage rate is ending soon

Many lenders allow you to secure a new deal up to six months before your current rate ends, helping you avoid moving onto higher standard rates.

You want to see if you could reduce your monthly payments

Reviewing your mortgage could reveal better rates or more suitable products based on your current circumstances.

You're self-employed or have complex income

Different lenders assess self-employed income in different ways. Expert packaging can make a significant difference to approval chances.

You're planning to move home

Understanding your borrowing position before making offers helps you move quickly and confidently when the right property appears.

You simply want clear guidance

A short conversation can often answer questions about deposits, affordability and lender criteria before you make big decisions.

If you're unsure where you stand, a quick conversation can give you clarity and help you plan the next steps with confidence.

Mortgage Advice FAQs

If you're thinking about using a mortgage broker, these are some of the most common questions we're asked. If you need tailored advice, we're here to help.

What does a mortgage broker do?

A mortgage broker searches the market on your behalf, compares suitable lenders and recommends options based on your circumstances. At Bay Mortgages, we guide you from your initial enquiry through to completion and help manage the paperwork and lender communication along the way.

Read more: What does a mortgage broker do?

How much deposit do I need for a mortgage?

Many lenders require a minimum deposit of around 5% to 10%, although this can vary depending on your credit history, income and the type of mortgage you need. A larger deposit can often unlock better rates.

Read more: How much deposit do I need?

Do mortgage brokers save you money?

A mortgage broker may save you money by finding competitive rates and helping you compare the total cost properly. We also help you avoid costly mistakes by explaining fees, terms and risks clearly before you commit.

Read more: Do mortgage brokers save you money?
