Buy-to-let FAQs

Buy-to-Let Mortgage FAQs

Here are some of the most common questions we get from landlords and property investors.

How much deposit do I need for a buy-to-let mortgage?

Most lenders require at least a 25% deposit, though some may accept 20%. A larger deposit can help secure better rates.

Can I get a buy-to-let mortgage as a first-time landlord?

Yes, some lenders accept first-time landlords, though criteria can be stricter. We'll help you find lenders open to new investors.

What is a limited company buy-to-let mortgage?

Instead of buying in your personal name, you purchase through a limited company (SPV). This can have tax advantages, but not all lenders offer it.

How is affordability assessed for buy-to-let?

Lenders use a rental income stress test, often requiring the rent to cover 125–145% of the mortgage payment.

Can I live in a buy-to-let property?

No — buy-to-let mortgages are for rental properties only. If you plan to live in the property, you'll need a standard residential mortgage.
